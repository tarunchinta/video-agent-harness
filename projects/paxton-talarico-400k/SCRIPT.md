# "The 400,000 Missing Votes" — Script Draft

Short-form video (~80 seconds). Paxton vs. Talarico, Texas U.S. Senate, November 2026.
Voiceover: Ty (audio in `audio/`). Word-level timestamps via `scripts/transcription.py`.

---

## BEAT 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:06)

**VO:** Texas might turn blue in November, because 400,000 Republicans disappeared in May.

**Visual:** Texas outline, blue bleeding in from the edges. Hard cut to a single voter silhouette.

---

## BEAT 2 — FOLLOW THE VOTER (0:06–0:18)

**VO:** Take one of them. March 3rd — he votes for John Cornyn. 910,000 Republicans do the same. Cornyn beats Ken Paxton by a point: 42 to 40.5. Then May 26th, the runoff. He stays home.

**Visual:** Calendar flip March → May. A ballot marked Cornyn. His dot on the Texas map goes dark.

---

## BEAT 3 — THE NUMBERS (0:18–0:35)

**VO:** Paxton wins in a landslide: 64 to 36, 28 points. His count barely moved from 883,000. Cornyn's fell by 400,000 — down 45 percent. Cornyn lost 400,000 votes, and Paxton gained none of them.

**Visual:** Twin bars for March; in May, Paxton's bar sits frozen while Cornyn's drains by nearly half. "−45%" callout.

---

## BEAT 4 — WHY (0:35–0:50)

**VO:** A week before the runoff, Trump endorses Paxton. Cornyn's city vote collapses — Paxton's margin swings 30 points in Houston, 40 in El Paso. The establishment stayed home, and the base took the party.

**Visual:** Trump endorsement headline screenshot lands in sync with the name-drop; animated city margin-swing chart.

---

## BEAT 5 — NOVEMBER MATH (0:50–1:08)

**VO:** Now zoom out. Last November, 11.4 million Texans voted. 400,000 is three and a half points of that — about the lead Talarico holds in the polls right now. And midterms draw fewer voters, so those 400,000 count for even more.

**Visual:** 11.4M bar with the 400K slice highlighted; poll graphic — Talarico 49, Paxton 45.

---

## BEAT 6 — CLOSER (1:08–1:18)

**VO:** May already happened. November comes down to whether 400,000 Republicans show up.

**Visual:** The voter's dark dot, flickering. End card: "Early voting starts tomorrow — Oct 19."

## Notes

- The "one voter" is an archetype representing the 400,000, not a real individual.
- The "3.5 points ≈ polling margin" rhyme is arithmetic, not a prediction. 2026 turnout is unknown; midterm electorates run smaller than presidential ones, which makes the 400K bloc loom larger, not smaller.
