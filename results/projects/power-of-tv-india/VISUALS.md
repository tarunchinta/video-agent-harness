# Visual Ideas — "The Soap Opera Effect"

## BEAT 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08)
- A village lane at dusk; one woman at a doorway (illustrated or AI-generated, never a real person).
- A cable line gets strung across the street above her.
- Caption: "Rural India, 2001".

## BEAT 2 — Turn (0:08–0:22)
- Three survey cards flip in sequence: "Need permission to go to the market?", "Is it OK for a husband to beat his wife?", "Want your next child to be a son?"
- Each card's tally slides toward the "after cable" side. Direction only, no invented percentages.

## BEAT 3 — How they know (0:22–0:37)
- Grid of 180 dots (villages). 65 light up for 2001.
- Year ticker 2001 → 2002 → 2003; more dots light up; the rest stay dark.
- Split into two groups, "got cable" vs. "no cable", with arrows over time.
- Small label: "≈2,700 households".

## BEAT 4 — Rehook (0:37–0:49)
- A speech bubble reading "the right answer" dissolves.
- Bar: girls enrolled in school, ticking up.
- Family timeline with births as dots; the gaps stretch apart.

## BEAT 5 — Why TV (0:49–1:10)
- "≈ 5 years of school": a stack of textbooks builds up.
- A TV inside the village home plays a stylised city drama: an office, a ledger, a woman at a desk. No real show footage.
- Lower third: "Kyunki Saas Bhi Kabhi Bahu Thi · Star Plus · 2000".

## BEAT 6 — Caveats (1:10–1:25)
- Card 1: "Not randomized", with two pre-trend lines running parallel before cable arrives.
- Card 2: "Replication: Iversen & Palmer-Jones, 2014", with one bar for women with schooling (effect) and one flat bar for women without.

## BEAT 7 — Closer (1:25–1:30)
- Back to the woman at the doorway; TV glow on her face. She steps out toward the market.
- End card: "Jensen & Oster, The Power of TV, QJE 2009".

## Assets needed
- Village scene + woman (illustrated or AI-generated)
- Stylised TV-drama inserts (no licensed show footage unless cleared)
- Dot-grid and bar animations (fluid, morphing, like the 400K project)
- Paper title card; replication citation card
- Voiceover recording (+ word-level SRT via `scripts/transcription.py`)
