# Skill: emotional-hook-first

## When to use
Opening any explainer video. Before a single technical fact appears on screen.

## How to apply
1. Pick a specific person doing a specific thing (not "people", not "the internet").
2. State the scope or time-box up front to open a curiosity gap ("what happens in the ~200 milliseconds after...").
3. Do not explain anything in the hook. Stakes first, exposition later.

## Example
Teardown 2026-10-18 (@charles.zhengg, Pacific photo reel): opens on a mother in
Shanghai sending a photo to her child in Los Angeles, with a 200ms time-box —
not on fiber optics or packets.

## Anti-pattern
Opening with a definition or a statistic. If the first line could belong to a
Wikipedia article, it is not a hook.
