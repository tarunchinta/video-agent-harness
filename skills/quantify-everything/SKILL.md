# Skill: quantify-everything

## When to use
Whenever a claim risks being an adjective ("fast", "huge", "tiny").

## How to apply
1. Convert each abstract step into a number: 16M bits, 1,500 packets, 12,000 km,
   200,000 km/s, 60 ms.
2. Prefer number-pairs that let the viewer do the math (distance + speed, not
   "very fast").
3. Add one mechanical texture detail per beat (repeaters every 80-100 km) to
   make the invisible tangible.
4. Treat numbers as visual elements: each one is something to show, not just say.

## Example
Teardown 2026-10-18 (@charles.zhengg, Pacific photo reel): the transit beat
gives distance, speed, repeater interval, and arrival time — four numbers, zero
adjectives.

## Anti-pattern
Adjective stacking ("incredibly fast over vast distances"). If the viewer
cannot picture a number, the claim is decoration.
