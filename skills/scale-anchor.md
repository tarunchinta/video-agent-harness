# Skill: scale-anchor

## When to use
Whenever a number is too big or too small to intuit.

## How to apply
1. Pair the abstract number with a body-scale physical reference the viewer
   already understands (thinner than a human hair, 4,000 meters deep).
2. Engineer tiny-vs-vast collisions: the smaller the reference and the larger
   the claim, the more memorable the beat.
3. One anchor per number. Two anchors compete; zero anchors float away.

## Example
Teardown 2026-10-18 (@charles.zhengg, Pacific photo reel): fiber thinner than a
human hair carrying 99% of intercontinental data. Tiny vs vast in one sentence.

## Anti-pattern
Naked big numbers ("12,000 kilometers of cable") with nothing to hold onto, or
piling on three comparisons for one number.
