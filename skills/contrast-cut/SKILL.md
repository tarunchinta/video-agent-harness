# Skill: contrast-cut

## When to use
After establishing a modern marvel, to make its scale legible. Baselines make
numbers mean something.

## How to apply
1. Pick one historical baseline as a number-pair (1858: 16 hours for 98 words).
2. Place it directly after the modern sequence with no commentary — let the gap
   argue.
3. Keep it to one cut. A history lesson kills the momentum the contrast created.

## Example
Teardown 2026-10-18 (@charles.zhengg, Pacific photo reel): immediately after the
60ms Pacific crossing, one line — the 1858 transatlantic telegraph took 16
hours to send 98 words.

## Anti-pattern
Explaining why the contrast matters ("this shows how far we've come"). If you
have to explain the gap, pick a sharper baseline.
