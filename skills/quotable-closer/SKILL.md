# Skill: quotable-closer

## When to use
The final line of the video. This is the distribution mechanism — what viewers
repeat to others.

## How to apply
1. Reframe everything shown in one sentence (engineering marvel -> coordination
   miracle).
2. Write it to be quoted: self-contained, slightly philosophical, no jargon.
3. End on meaning, not facts. The last taste should be an idea, not a number.

## Example
Teardown 2026-10-18 (@charles.zhengg, Pacific photo reel): "the internet works
because billions of independently owned machines follow a shared agreement to
pass data onward."

## Anti-pattern
Ending on a summary ("so that's how the internet works!") or a CTA. Summaries
are forgotten; reframes are repeated.
